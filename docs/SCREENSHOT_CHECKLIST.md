# DineEase Screenshot Checklist

This checklist assigns each team member the specific features they are responsible for capturing. Ensure that the screenshots clearly demonstrate the functionality working in the browser. 

Save your screenshots in the respective `docs/screenshots/FXX/` folders using clear names (e.g., `F01_menu_list.png`, `F01_menu_details.png`).

---

- [ ] **F02 Search & Filter**: Capture the search bar returning specific results and category filters applied.
- [ ] **F06 Favourite Menu Items**: Capture the user's favorites list and the "heart" toggle on a menu item.
- [ ] **F07 Reviews & Ratings**: Capture a review being submitted and the reviews displayed under a menu item.
- [ ] **F20 Loyalty Points**: Capture the loyalty points display on the customer dashboard and the checkout redemption option.
